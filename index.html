Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents uploaded when using the online tools?

No. All online tools on this website process your files 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and HTML5 Canvas APIs. Your documents, photos, and signatures are never uploaded to any remote server or external service, ensuring complete privacy.

Privacy Policy & Guarantees

1. Zero Document Storage

AAYAM Innovation provides free online tools designed to process data locally within your web browser. When you use our Image Converters, Image Resizer, or Text Case Converter, your data is processed entirely in client-side memory using HTML5 APIs. No files, text, or images are transmitted to our servers or stored externally.

2. Android Applications

Our Android applications are built in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. Permissions requested by each application (such as camera access for AAYAM Scanner or storage access for AAYAM Office Pro) are utilized solely to provide the specified functional capability on your device.

3. Analytics & Cookies

This static website does not track personal browsing habits or install invasive third-party advertising cookies. We believe in high utility without sacrificing user privacy.

4. Contact Inquiries

Information submitted through our contact form is used strictly to respond to user requests, bug reports, and partnership inquiries.
